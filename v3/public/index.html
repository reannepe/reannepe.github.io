About

Licensed architect with experience across architecture, interiors, visualization, and project coordination.

Reanne Denise Pe is a PRC-licensed architect based in Metro Manila, Philippines. Her work spans residential and commercial design, architectural interiors, 3D visualization, technical drawing support, and practical coordination between design intent and construction needs.

She is strongest when a project needs both design sensitivity and technical follow-through: believable renderings, clear presentation sheets, practical drafting support, and thoughtful interior direction grounded in buildable decisions.

Typical Workflow

From brief to presentation-ready package

  1. Review the brief, references, site information, and constraints.
  2. Build mood boards, references, and early layouts.
  3. Develop floor plans, models, and schematic visuals.
  4. Refine renderings, materials, and presentation graphics.
  5. Prepare organized source files, boards, drawings, and supporting documents.

Typical Turnaround

Fast on focused scopes, flexible on larger packages

  • ADU massing study and site plan: 2-4 days
  • ADU floor plans and concept model: 3-5 days
  • Exterior render package: 3-7 days
  • Interior renovation render package: 2-5 days
  • Longer packages depend on project scale, revisions, and client response time
