Things About Me

A short introduction, in a more personal voice.

I’m a licensed architect based in Metro Manila, Philippines, and most of my work sits between architecture, interiors, visualization, and technical project support.

I like projects where I can help make ideas clearer, calmer, and easier to build, whether that means a rendering, a drawing set, a presentation board, or practical design decisions behind the scenes.

Credentials

  • BS Architecture — University of Santo Tomas
  • PRC Licensed Architect
  • UAP Member — Manila Atelier Chapter

Typical support

  • 3D visualization for interior and exterior work
  • Floor plans, detailing, and drawing packages
  • Architectural interior design and presentation support

How I Usually Help

These are the main ways I usually support a project.

3D Visualization

Interior and exterior renderings, presentation visuals, and modeling-only support when a project needs form, atmosphere, or design direction without a full drawing package.

Drawing Support

Drafting, floor plans, detailing, presentation sheets, architectural documents, and full-set drawing packages that can support the transition from design into construction.

Exterior Studies

Massing studies, facade development, layout problem-solving, and technical design support for projects that need both architectural thinking and visual clarity.
