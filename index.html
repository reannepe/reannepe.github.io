Things About Me

A short introduction, in a more personal voice.

I’m a licensed architect based in Metro Manila, Philippines, and most of my work sits between architecture, interiors, visualization, and technical project support.

I like projects where I can help make ideas clearer, calmer, and easier to build, whether that means a rendering, a drawing set, a presentation board, or practical design decisions behind the scenes.

Featured Projects

Curated case studies highlighting spatial problem-solving across scales.

01 • Urbina Residence

Reworking a cramped Makati studio into a warmer, more flexible home.

This condominium unit in Makati City started as a tight studio with existing dividers that made the space feel smaller and less usable. The concept removed old partitions and introduced a half-modular, half-glass divider strategy combined with storage-focused furniture planning. Light built-ins with wood and fabric contrast, carpet zoning, mirrors, and glass were used to enlarge the perceived space.

  • Architectural interior design + project management
  • Half-modular, half-glass divider strategy
  • 5-week design-build schedule

02 • One Florida Place Dormitel

Balancing room capacity, circulation, and presentation across a 6-storey concept.

A multi-storey dormitel in Valenzuela City requiring varied room types, commercial space, parking, and a stronger market presence, all within budget and lot constraints. The project maximized room count and variety while balancing cost, ventilation, commercial income spaces, and penthouse requirements.

  • Overall project management + exterior and interior design
  • Maximized room count within budget and lot constraints
  • 32-week project timeline

03 • Ready, Set, Learn

Child-friendly planning and Scandinavian-inspired interiors for a small playschool fit-out.

Designed for a Quezon City playschool and therapy center with a playful, modern Scandinavian interior. The project solved exposed piping and limited floor area using muted pastel colors, light wood textures, fun fixtures, foldable wall panels, hidden built-in storage, decorative ceiling panels, and glass dividers.

  • Full drawing set: plans, sections, elevations, schedules, BOM
  • Signed-and-sealed blueprints
  • 3-week design-only scope
