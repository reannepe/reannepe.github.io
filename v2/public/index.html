Case studies

Three projects that best explain Reanne’s design judgment and visualization strengths.

01 • Urbina Residence

Reworking a cramped studio into a warmer, more flexible home.

This condominium unit in Makati City started as a tight studio with existing dividers that made the space feel smaller and less usable. Reanne’s concept opened the unit visually while adding storage, zoning, and multi-functional furniture.

  • Scope: architectural interior design, project management, modular contractor coordination
  • Approach: half-modular and half-glass dividers, integrated storage, foldable pieces, warm lighting
  • Outcome focus: make a compact footprint feel calmer, larger, and more livable

02 • One Florida Place Dormitel

Balancing room capacity, circulation, ventilation, and presentation across a 6-storey concept.

The project required a multi-storey dormitel in Valenzuela City with varied room types, commercial space, parking, and a stronger market presence, all within budget and lot constraints.

  • Scope: architect, architectural interior designer, decorator, and project manager
  • Duration: 32-week design and project management scope
  • Strength demonstrated: handling both planning logic and presentation imagery in one workflow

03 • Ready, Set, Learn

Child-friendly planning and Scandinavian-inspired interiors for a small therapy and playschool fit-out.

Designed for a Quezon City playschool and therapy center, this concept had to feel playful, safe, welcoming, and budget-aware within a compact footprint.

  • Scope: architectural interior design
  • Approach: muted pastels, light wood textures, hidden storage, foldable wall panels, glass dividers
  • Challenge solved: exposed piping and limited floor area without losing warmth or flexibility
